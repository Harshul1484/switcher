# Code of Conduct

Switcher is a small project, and it should be a pleasant place to contribute. These rules apply in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith. People contribute in their spare time and in their second or third language.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific and actionable.
- Stay on topic, and accept the maintainer's decisions on scope and direction.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sharing anyone's private information, including credentials, tokens or account details.
- Spam, self-promotion, or repeatedly pushing for changes that were declined.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [Harshul Rathod](https://github.com/Harshul1484), may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules. Report a problem by contacting the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.
